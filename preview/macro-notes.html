Mostly Harmless Heterogeneous Macro

A Guided Tour to Theoretical Macroeconomics

March 2026

This note is a practical manual for closed-economy New Keynesian models with increasing degrees of household heterogeneity. It starts from the representative-agent benchmark, moves to a two-agent New Keynesian economy, then introduces a tractable heterogeneous-agent intermediate step—THANK—and culminates in a baseline HANK model with incomplete markets and idiosyncratic labor-income risk. The aim is strictly pedagogical. Every environment is presented with household and firm problems, first-order conditions, equilibrium restrictions, nominal rigidities, monetary and fiscal rules, market-clearing conditions, and computational logic. The exposition is intentionally slow. Definitions, timing conventions, log-linearization, and the role of firms, profits, fiscal incidence, and redistribution are all developed in detail so that students can move from intuition to equations and then to code without losing the thread.

Coverage

Mathematical foundations, log-linearization, the common New Keynesian firm and policy blocks, and a progressive comparison of RANK, TANK, THANK, and HANK. The final sections discuss computation, sequence-space methods, fiscal structure, and sticky wages.
