Mostly Harmless Heterogeneous Macro

A Guided Tour to Theoretical Macroeconomics

March 2026

This note is a practical manual for closed-economy New Keynesian models with increasing degrees of household heterogeneity. It starts from the representative-agent benchmark, moves to a two-agent New Keynesian economy, then introduces a tractable heterogeneous-agent intermediate step—THANK—and culminates in a baseline HANK model with incomplete markets and idiosyncratic labor-income risk. The aim is strictly pedagogical. Every environment is presented with household and firm problems, first-order conditions, equilibrium restrictions, nominal rigidities, monetary and fiscal rules, market-clearing conditions, and computational logic.

Applied report

Capital público e infraestructuras en España

August 2026 · Spanish

This report extends the public-capital accounting exercise to Spain. Combining IMF–AMECO and BBVA–Ivie data, it documents a peak in aggregate public capital in 2012, a subsequent decline, and the composition of that erosion across infrastructure assets and regions. A calibrated production framework translates the capital gap into a range of implications for GDP per capita.
