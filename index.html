Hi, I’m Jorge.

I’m a Spanish economist, a research assistant at the KOF Swiss Economic Institute and an incoming PhD student at ETH Zurich.

My interests lie in international macroeconomics, global shocks and international trade. I’m especially interested in how shocks spread across countries and affect households and firms.

Outside economics, I enjoy football, Formula 1, tennis and chess.
