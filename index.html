Welcome to my website.

Hi, my name is Jorge and I’m a Spanish economist. I’m a PhD student at ETH Zurich and a doctoral researcher at the KOF Swiss Economic Institute.

My interests lie in international macroeconomics, global shocks and international trade. I’m especially interested in how shocks spread across countries and affect households and firms.

Outside economics, I’m a big sports fan: I enjoy going to the gym and skiing, and I follow football, tennis and Formula 1. I like chess, visiting museums and going for a walk. I’m interested in other disciplines such as philosophy and history.
